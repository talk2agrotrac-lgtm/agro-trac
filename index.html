Our Mission

Closing the gap in access to farm inputs

AgroTrac is a startup founded and run by young people from Sokoto. We started it because local farmers, especially smallholders, were being locked out of the machinery that lets a farm grow past manual, subsistence-scale work. Renting instead of owning puts a tractor, a harvester, or an irrigation set within reach of a farmer who couldn't otherwise afford one, so they can plant, harvest, and grow at scale. We've since grown outlets across the northern states, and previously partnered with Trotro Tractors in Jigawa to extend that access further.
